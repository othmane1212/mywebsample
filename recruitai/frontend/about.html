Why RecruitAI exists

Hiring is broken on both sides of the table. We built the fix for both.

Recruiters drown in applications that all start to look the same after the fiftieth one. Candidates apply into a void and never learn why they didn't make the cut. RecruitAI exists to fix both problems with the same tool, at the same time.

What's in it for you

Two very different people. One shared problem: not enough signal.

Recruiters

Get your evenings back

Every application is scored the moment it lands. You open your dashboard already sorted best-fit-first, with the reasoning shown, not a mystery number. You stop reading resumes to find the good ones and start reading resumes to decide who to call.

Candidates

Know where you actually stand

No more submitting into silence. You see your match score, which of your skills counted, and what you're missing for a role, before or after you apply. Rejection stops being a mystery and starts being information you can act on.

Everyone

A process that explains itself

Every score comes with a plain-language reason. Not a black box, not a "the algorithm decided." If a person on either side asks "why," the platform already has the answer written down.

Where AI actually fits in

The AI's job is narrow on purpose: read, compare, explain. Nothing more.

It reads for meaning, not keywords

The matching engine compares resume language against the job description directly, so a candidate who describes their experience differently than the job posting is worded still gets recognized as relevant.

It weighs what recruiters actually care about

Required skills count more than nice-to-haves. Experience is judged against what the role needs, not treated as "more is always better." These are rules a recruiter would apply by hand, just applied consistently to every single application.

It never makes the final call

RecruitAI ranks and explains. It doesn't reject anyone, hire anyone, or hide anyone from view. Every decision, every interview invite, every rejection, is made by a human. The AI's entire job is making sure that human isn't starting from zero.

That's the whole idea. Less guessing, more talking to actual people.
