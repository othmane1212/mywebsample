Understanding the terms

Titulaire, Contractuel, Concours, what they actually mean here

Titulaire

Poste permanent

A permanent civil-servant teaching position, typically filled through the national recruitment competition (concours). Job security and status within the public education system.

Contractuel

Poste par contrat

A fixed-term teaching contract, often regional, filled through direct recruitment rather than the national competition. Faster to fill, renewable, distinct legal status from a titulaire post.

Concours

Concours national

The national recruitment exam. Candidates who've passed it can flag this on their profile, recruiters can require it when a position calls for it, it's checked automatically, not just written in the job description.
